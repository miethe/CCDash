Recovered from the MeatySkills lineage: `SPEC.md`, `LICENSE`, the 2026-05-29 transport-tag correction pass, and 8 router intents absent from the skillmeat lineage (`feature-report`, `artifact-rankings`, `artifact-recommendations`, `live-active-count`, `system-active-count`, `project-add`, `project-list`, `project-use`). - **router-table.json v2.3 — 33 intents** (union of 29 + 25, no entry dropped). Transport tags were re-derived against ground truth rather than trusted from either lineage, and against **both** CLI surfaces: `backend/cli/commands/*.py` (in-repo) and `packages/ccdash_cli/src/ccdash_cli/commands/*.py` (standalone), at CCDash `670bcc9`. The in-repo CLI registers exactly `feature report`, `session search|get|transcript`, `artifact rankings|recommendations`, `live active-count`, `system active-count`, `report aar|aar-review`, `status project`, `workflow failures`. Corrected the ambiguous `["cli"]` transport the fork reintroduced on `session-search` -> `["mcp","in-repo-cli","standalone-cli"]` and on `session-detail`/`session-transcript` -> `["mcp","in-repo-cli"]`.
- **Correction made during this reconciliation, recorded because the wrong version briefly existed**: `report-feature` (`ccdash report feature <feature_id>`, carried only by the skillmeat lineage) was first ruled a phantom and dropped, on the basis that the in-repo `report` group exposes only `aar` and `aar-review`. That was wrong — it is a **real standalone-CLI command** (`packages/ccdash_cli/src/ccdash_cli/commands/report.py`, `@report_app.command("feature")`). It is restored with `transport: ["standalone-cli"]`. The lesson is the one this whole reconciliation is about: **CCDash has two CLI surfaces, and verifying a command against only one of them manufactures phantoms.** A related bad edit in `references/command-feature.md` (rewriting a correct `report feature` reference to `feature report`) was reverted for the same reason.
- **Known staleness, NOT fixed here** (recorded so it is not mistaken for coverage): both lineages predate current CCDash. The in-repo CLI now also registers `report aar-review` and the `routing`, `persona`, `provider`, `token`, and `research_run` groups, none of which appear in this router table or the references. Re-aligning the skill to the shipped runtime is separate work.
